About Us

The Iron Lions FTC Team 24089 is a passionate group of students driven by innovation, teamwork, and STEM excellence. We strive to build not only competitive robots but also foster leadership and community engagement through FIRST Tech Challenge. Our dedication spans designing, programming, and competing with resilience and creativity.

Sponsors

Thank you to our sponsors for your continuous support and belief in our mission. Your generosity empowers us to innovate, compete, and inspire the next generation of STEM leaders.

Achievements

APOC Champions

After months of rigorous preparation and teamwork, the Iron Lions proudly secured first place at the prestigious Asia-Pacific Open Championship. Our robot demonstrated exceptional consistency, dominating both autonomous and driver-controlled phases, a testament to our programming excellence and strategic design.

Inspire Award at APOC

Winning the Inspire Award at APOC highlights our commitment beyond robotics, recognizing excellence in team outreach, community involvement, and gracious professionalism. This award reflects our dedication to inspiring others and building a positive FIRST culture.

2nd Place - Australia Nationals

Our strong performance at the Australia Nationals earned us second place, showcasing our ability to compete at a high level nationally. The experience strengthened our skills and fueled our passion for continuous improvement and excellence.
